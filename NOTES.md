# Study notes
1. First, it must plainly be a one-line pigeon pitch to investors; anything off-brief loses immediately.
2. Then the joke must land; a mild pun or a straight description is not enough.
3. Original surprise breaks ties next; avoid the thousandth crumbs/statues/poop variation.
4. Keep city-pigeon life unmistakable, then make every word earn its place under 200 characters.
5. Rivals will mostly say “Uber for breadcrumbs,” “disrupt statues,” or “pivot to poop.”
6. Better angles: pigeons remember human faces; flock behavior turns one crumb into a market; odd pigeon biology/one-foot sleep becomes real business jargon.
